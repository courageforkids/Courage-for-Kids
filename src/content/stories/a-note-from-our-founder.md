When I first founded Courage for Kids, I did so with a simple belief: no child facing cancer should ever feel alone.

What started as an idea quickly became something much bigger, a community of people united by compassion, generosity, and a desire to make a difference in the lives of children and families navigating one of the most difficult journeys imaginable.

At Courage for Kids, we believe support goes beyond treatment. It’s found in the moments of encouragement, the acts of kindness, and the communities that choose to stand beside families when they need it most. Every initiative we launch, every partnership we build, and every goal we pursue is rooted in that belief.

As we continue to grow, our mission remains the same: to bring comfort, hope, and support to children battling cancer and the families who fight alongside them. Whether you’re a donor, volunteer, supporter, partner, or simply someone who believes in our mission, you are an important part of this story.

Thank you for believing in our mission and for helping us create a future where no family faces pediatric cancer alone.

*Their fight. Our mission.*
