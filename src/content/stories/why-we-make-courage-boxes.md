## Treatment changes more than routines

Appointments and hospital stays can interrupt school, friendships, play, and everyday life. For a child, cancer changes far more than a calendar.

## Play is more than just play

Creative activities and age-appropriate play can help children cope with stressful healthcare experiences. Something to make, build, or color can turn a long afternoon into a moment that feels like being a kid again.

## Comfort looks different for everyone

A familiar blanket, a fun activity, or a thoughtful gesture can offer moments of connection and reassurance. Comfort doesn’t cure, but it can care.

## That’s why we create Courage Boxes

Courage Boxes are thoughtful comfort items for children facing cancer and their families, packed by our youth-led team and delivered with the message that a whole community is cheering them on.
